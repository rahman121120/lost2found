Slide 2 — Problem Statement
Problem

Every year, students lose valuable belongings such as wallets, ID cards, mobile phones, calculators, and documents on campus.

Current recovery methods rely on:

WhatsApp groups
Word of mouth
Notice boards
Manual reporting

These methods are unorganized, slow, and often lead to unsuccessful recovery.

Slide 3 — Proposed Solution
Lost2Found

Lost2Found provides a centralized platform where students and staff can:

Report lost items
Report found items
Search existing reports
Submit claim requests
Recover belongings faster

The system improves communication while protecting user privacy.
